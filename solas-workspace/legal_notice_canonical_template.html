RECITALS

WHEREAS, Othaiim LLC is the developer and owner of certain proprietary artificial intelligence and recursive self-improvement ("AI/RSI") technology, including the AIIM governance framework, for which patent protection is currently pending under United States Patent and Trademark Office Application No. 1135-11714-1 (the "Patent Application");

WHEREAS, Iconic Machinery is an independently operated, duly authorized heavy equipment dealership that utilizes certain technology licensed or made available by Othaiim LLC in the ordinary course of its dealer operations;

WHEREAS, various individuals, contractors, and representatives have, in the course of business, been granted limited access to systems, applications, and data associated with the foregoing entities;

WHEREAS, Othaiim LLC is engaged in preliminary discussions with Base44, Inc. ("Base44") regarding a prospective partnership for the licensed use of the Base44 platform as an enterprise infrastructure base, which partnership remains subject to the negotiation and execution of a definitive written agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, Othaiim LLC issues this Amended Legal Notice to establish, memorialize, and clarify the ownership structure, confidentiality obligations, and current status of all rights described herein.

ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS AND ENTITY SEPARATION

1.1 "Iconic Machinery" means the duly authorized Bobcat dealership engaged in the sale, rental, and servicing of heavy equipment. Iconic Machinery is, and at all times has been, a dealer operations entity, and is the sole and exclusive owner of all equipment inventory data, dealer books and records, customer records, sales transaction records, dealership trade name, dealership logo, and associated trade dress (collectively, the "Dealer Assets").

1.2 "Othaiim LLC" means the technology and intellectual property holding entity that owns, develops, and governs the AI/RSI Operating System, including without limitation all software architecture, backend and frontend source code, user interface and user experience designs, database schemas, proprietary algorithms, artificial intelligence and machine learning models, three-dimensional digital twin pipelines, the AIIM governance framework, and all associated intellectual property, whether registered, unregistered, or pending registration (collectively, the "Platform IP"). Othaiim LLC is the applicant and owner of record with respect to the Patent Application, which remains PATENT PENDING and has not, as of the Effective Date, been issued as a granted patent.

1.3 Separateness of Entities. Othaiim LLC and Iconic Machinery are, and shall at all times be treated as, separate and legally distinct entities. Othaiim LLC does not own, and expressly disclaims, any right, title, or interest in the Dealer Assets. Iconic Machinery does not own, and expressly disclaims, any right, title, or interest in the Platform IP, including the Patent Application and any patent that may issue therefrom. No act, omission, or course of dealing between the parties shall be construed to merge, blend, or create joint ownership between these two entities.

ARTICLE II — OWNERSHIP STATEMENT

2.1 Dealer Assets. All equipment inventory data, stock numbers, serial numbers, pricing data, dealer cost records, customer profiles, sales representative records, dealership financial records, dealership location data, the Iconic Machinery trade name and logo, and all associated dealer operational data are, and shall remain, the sole and exclusive property of Iconic Machinery. No Dealer Asset is transferred, licensed, assigned, or otherwise conveyed to any third party by virtue of this Notice.

2.2 Platform IP. All software designs, user interface designs, frontend and backend source code, database schemas, application programming interfaces, proprietary algorithms, artificial intelligence and machine learning models, RSI datasets, the AIIM governance framework, TrustLayer systems, DOT/CBORB protocols, three-dimensional Gaussian Splat digital twin pipelines, voice-to-quote systems, quote engine logic, and all associated intellectual property — including all right, title, and interest in and to the Patent Application (USPTO 1135-11714-1, PATENT PENDING) and any patent(s) that may issue therefrom — are, and shall remain, the sole and exclusive property of Othaiim LLC. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.

2.3 Branding. The Iconic Machinery name, wordmark, logo, tagline ("Authorized Bobcat Dealer Since 1975"), trade dress, and associated visual identity are the sole property of Iconic Machinery. Othaiim LLC asserts no claim of ownership over dealership branding.

ARTICLE III — NO TRANSFER OF DATA OR OWNERSHIP

3.1 No Data Transfer to Base44 or Wix. No Dealer Asset, inventory data, customer data, dealer record, or any data owned by Iconic Machinery has been or shall be transferred, assigned, licensed, sold, or otherwise conveyed to Base44, Wix, Inc., or any other third party. This Notice expressly affirms that no transfer of data ownership has occurred, is occurring, or is implied by any prior communication.

3.2 No Transfer of Platform IP. No Platform IP owned by Othaiim LLC, including the Patent Application, has been or shall be transferred, assigned, or sold to Base44, Wix, Inc., or any other third party pursuant to this Notice. All Platform IP remains the sole and exclusive property of Othaiim LLC, with all rights reserved.

3.3 Current Securement — Licensing Only. The only rights presently secured in connection with Base44 are limited to preliminary AI/RSI licensing discussions for the limited purpose of evaluating the Base44 platform as an infrastructure base for enterprise deployment. Such discussions constitute, at most, a prospective licensing arrangement and do not constitute, and shall not be construed as, a transfer, assignment, or sale of any ownership right, data, intellectual property, or asset.

ARTICLE IV — FRONTEND DESIGN: TRANSFER PENDING

4.1 Status. The frontend designs of all applications comprising the Platform IP, including without limitation user interfaces, page layouts, component designs, visual workflows, and associated design assets (collectively, the "Frontend Designs"), are currently in a contemplated stage of ownership transfer. Any such transfer remains subject to, and contingent upon, the successful negotiation and execution of a definitive written agreement. No transfer shall be deemed effective absent such an agreement.

4.2 Waiver of Ownership by Users. Any individual who has accessed, viewed, interacted with, or contributed to the Frontend Designs or any component of the Platform IP hereby acknowledges and agrees that such individual holds no ownership right, claim, or interest therein. Access, use, contribution, or interaction with the Platform IP, by itself, does not create, vest, or convey any ownership interest of any kind. All such rights are, and shall remain, expressly reserved to Othaiim LLC.

4.3 No Implied Rights. No user, developer, contractor, representative, or other third party shall acquire any ownership interest, co-authorship right, or derivative work right in any Frontend Design or Platform IP by virtue of access, use, contribution, or interaction, whether express or implied, absent a separate written agreement duly executed by an authorized officer of Othaiim LLC.

ARTICLE V — BASE44 PARTNERSHIP: PENDING DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT

5.1 Status of Discussions. Othaiim LLC is engaged in preliminary partnership discussions with Base44 concerning the prospective use of the Base44 platform as an infrastructure base for enterprise deployment of the AI/RSI Operating System. Such discussions are exploratory and non-binding, and no partnership shall be deemed formed absent a definitive written agreement executed by both parties.

5.2 Interim Reservation of Rights. Pending execution of a definitive agreement, ALL RIGHTS, TITLE, AND INTEREST in and to the Platform IP, the Frontend Designs, prospective AI/RSI licensing rights, and all associated intellectual property are, and shall remain, expressly reserved to Othaiim LLC. No right is transferred, licensed, or assigned to Base44 or any other party by virtue of the existence of these discussions or this Notice.

5.3 Limited Scope. The contemplated scope of any partnership between Othaiim LLC and Base44 is limited to the use of the Base44 platform as a technological infrastructure base for the AI/RSI Operating System. No such partnership shall include, contemplate, or permit the transfer of any Dealer Asset owned by Iconic Machinery, including without limitation inventory data, customer data, or dealer operational records.

6.1 Confidential Information. All information accessed through the Platform IP, including without limitation software architecture, source code, database schemas, user interface designs, proprietary algorithms, AI/ML model outputs, RSI scores, TrustLayer records, DOT/CBORB protocols, and business or pricing logic, constitutes "Confidential Information" of Othaiim LLC and/or Iconic Machinery, as applicable.

6.2 Obligations. No recipient of Confidential Information shall disclose, share, copy, reproduce, reverse-engineer, decompile, distribute, or make any commercial use of such Confidential Information without the prior written consent of an authorized officer of Othaiim LLC.

6.3 Duration. The confidentiality obligations set forth herein shall survive in perpetuity, notwithstanding termination of access to the Platform IP or Dealer Assets.

6.4 Remedies. Any breach of this Article VI may result in immediate revocation of access, an action for damages, and/or injunctive relief.
